Why schools choose us

Built for real classrooms, not demo days.

Every design choice is shaped by the schools we serve: shaky Wi-Fi, old Chromebooks, mixed Android tablets, occasional power cuts.

Works offline

Service-worker cached. After first load, lessons and coding keep working through power cuts and Wi-Fi outages — only the optional AI helpers need a connection.

Built for low-spec hardware

Optimised for older Chromebooks, budget Android tablets and entry-level Windows laptops — the cheap devices schools actually buy.

No installs, nothing to deploy

Pure web. IT teams don't need to image machines or push packages. Students open a URL and start building.

Year-end certification

Students who complete the year programme earn a completion certificate issued by ZeroAI Technologies, an IBM Business Partner — a real credential for their record.

Teacher controls built in

Classroom-control tooling lets teachers steer the whole lab — push lessons, run competitions, see who's stuck.

Competitions & champions

Daily challenges, classroom races and a school leaderboard keep the lab competitive — winners go up on the Champions board.

About us

Zambian-founded. Built where the constraints are real.

ZeroAI Technologies was founded in 2014. Twelve years of building software, robotics and AI-automation systems — for two years now focused intensively on K-12 STEM, after our founder returned full-time from his earlier work in school robotics programmes.

We're PACRA-registered in Zambia and MSME-registered in India. The platform is built from Punjab; in the Philippines our partner Apple Tutors handles in-person delivery from Davao. Our first long-term school partnership — Drishti Public School in Ludhiana — went live February 2026, and we're actively engaging schools across India, the Philippines, the Gulf, Switzerland and the UK.
