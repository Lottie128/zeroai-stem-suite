Why schools choose us

Built for real classrooms, not fancy demos.

Every design choice is shaped by the schools we serve: shaky Wi-Fi, old Chromebooks, mixed Android tablets, occasional power cuts.

Works offline

Service-worker cached. After first load, every app keeps running through power cuts and Wi-Fi outages.

Under 1 GB RAM

Tested on Raspberry Pi Zero, 6-year-old Chromebooks and ₹6,000 Android tablets. If it boots a browser, it runs the suite.

No installs

Pure web. IT teams don't need to deploy anything. Students open the URL and start coding.

One license, five apps

Pay once at the school level. Every student, every grade, every app — no per-seat counting.

About us

Zambian-founded. Built where the constraints are real.

ZeroAI Technologies was founded in 2014. Twelve years of building software, robotics and AI-automation systems — for two years now focused intensively on K-12 STEM, after our founder returned full-time from his earlier work in school robotics programmes.

We're PACRA-registered in Zambia and MSME-registered in India. We build the apps from Punjab, deliver pilots in Lusaka, Davao, Cebu, Ludhiana and the Gulf, and ship the work that fits the schools we actually visit.
